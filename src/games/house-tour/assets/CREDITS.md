# Assets

Scanned surfaces and the sky are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), licensed CC0. They were resized and re-encoded as WebP.

- `tatami-*`: [Tatami Mat](https://polyhaven.com/a/tatami_mat)
- `floor-*`: [Laminate Floor 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/laminate_floor_02)
- `oak-*`: [Oak Veneer 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01)
- `walnut-*`: [Walnut Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/walnut_veneer)
- `whiteoak-*`: [White Oak Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_oak_veneer)
- `hinoki-*`: [Hinoki Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/hinoki_planks)
- `cedar-*`: [Japanese Cedar Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/japanese_cedar_planks)
- `clay-*`: [Clay Plaster](https://polyhaven.com/a/clay_plaster)
- `granite-*`: [Granite Tile](https://polyhaven.com/a/granite_tile)
- `concrete-*`: [Concrete Floor 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02)
- `gravel-*`: [Gravel Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_floor)
- `grass-*`: [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass)
- `asphalt-*`: [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02)
- `deck-*`: [Wood Floor Deck](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor_deck)
- `linen-*`: [Rough Linen](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen)
- `boucle-*`: [Wool Boucle](https://polyhaven.com/a/wool_boucle)
- `sky.webp` and `sky-light.hdr`: [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky); the background keeps only the upper hemisphere.

Everything else is generated in code: the house, kawara roof tiles, siding, wallpaper, fusuma and shoji paper, the hanging scroll, furniture, plants, the car and the street. The thumbnail is captured from the running game.
