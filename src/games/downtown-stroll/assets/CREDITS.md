# Assets

Scanned surfaces and the sky are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), licensed CC0. They were resized and re-encoded as WebP; the sidewalk pavers were desaturated.

- `asphalt-*`: [Asphalt 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_04)
- `pavers-*`: [Square Concrete Pavers](https://polyhaven.com/a/square_concrete_pavers)
- `brick-*`: [Red Brick 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/red_brick_03)
- `concrete-*`: [Concrete Wall 008](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_008)
- `sandstone-*`: [White Sandstone Bricks 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_sandstone_bricks_03)
- `grass-*`: [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass)
- `sky.webp` and `sky-light.hdr`: [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky); the background keeps only the upper hemisphere.

Everything else, including buildings, window interiors, cars, people, trees, and signs, is generated in code. The thumbnail is captured from the running game.
